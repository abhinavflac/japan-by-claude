# 24 Cameras, One Living Japanese Street

One Tokyo shōtengai street at blue hour, just after the rain, built once in three.js and filmed by 24 physical cameras in the same world. Traffic, signals, crowds and the man under the sign all run on one deterministic clock, so every camera sees the same moment. Cinematic transitions, a free explore camera, a walk mode where you play a person in the street (W), a day mode in a morning sun shower (N), nine looks from noir to thermal (V), a minimap, ambient sound, and an offline film exporter.

- Open `index.html` in Chrome, or rebuild it from `src/` with `node build.mjs`.
- Export the film with `node tools/film.mjs` (needs ffmpeg and a GPU).

See `CLAUDE.md` for the architecture, testing hooks and roadmap.
